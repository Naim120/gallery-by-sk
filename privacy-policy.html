Privacy Policy for Gallery by SK

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Gallery by SK ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Gallery by SK is a local media viewer and editor that offers a cloud backup feature using Google Drive. The app accesses your device's storage locally to display your photos and videos.

3. Google Drive Integration & Scopes

If you choose to use the Cloud Backup feature, our app requests access to your Google Drive using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope.

How we use this data: We strictly use this scope to upload and download your backed-up media files to a hidden, dedicated application data folder on your Google Drive. This ensures your gallery backups are kept separate from your personal Google Drive files, maintaining the integrity of your folder structure.

Data Privacy: We do NOT access, view, share, or transmit your media files to any third-party servers. The transfer of data occurs entirely between your Android device and your personal Google Drive account.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You can delete your cloud backups at any time directly from within the app using the "Delete" option in the Cloud tab, which will permanently remove the application data folder from your Google Drive.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository where this project is hosted.
